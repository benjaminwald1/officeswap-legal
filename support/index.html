Support

We're here to help with OfficeSwap.

Contact us

Email support@officeswap.co with any question, problem or feedback. We aim to reply within a few business days.

What to include

  • Your organization's name. Please don't send your access code.
  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if something looks wrong.

Common questions

Many questions are answered in the FAQ, including how matching works, why someone might still be on the waitlist, and how to cancel.
