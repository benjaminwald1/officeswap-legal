An iPhone app for sharing offices within one organization, where some people have their own office and others don't. Office holders offer their office on the days they're away, colleagues without an office request one, and the app matches them automatically. Offices are only ever shared with people in your own organization.

Open the app and enter your organization's access code. Ask whoever set up OfficeSwap for your team if you don't have it. There's no account, email or password.

Go to officeswap.co/start, or tap Register an organization in the app, which opens it. Choose a plan, enter your organization's name and office address, and add a payment method. Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial. You get your access code right away. Then scan your staff list or type in your offices, and share the code with your colleagues.

Joining your organization is free for everyone. Setting up a new organization is a monthly subscription that starts with a 30-day free trial: Starter is $299 a month for up to 19 offices, Growth is $699 for up to 99, and Enterprise is $1,099 for 100 or more. Cancel anytime. See Billing & Refunds.
