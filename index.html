Questions? Answered.

What is OfficeSwap?

An iPhone app for sharing offices within one organization, where some people have their own office and others don't. Office holders offer their office on the days they're away, colleagues without an office request one, and the app matches them automatically. Offices are only ever shared with people in your own organization.

How does OfficeSwap help?

It puts the offices your company already has to use. When someone is out, their office goes to a colleague who needs one instead of sitting empty. New hires, part-timers and coworkers visiting from another location get a quiet room, office holders keep theirs every other day, and nobody has to chase emails or spreadsheets to find a space.

Do I have to type in every office?

No. In Settings, scan a printed staff list or directory with your iPhone camera. OfficeSwap reads each name, office number and photo, and you check the list before anything is added.

How do I get an office?

On the Board, tap Request an Office, choose your name and pick the days you'll be in. If an office is free on those days, it's assigned to you right away.

What happens if nothing is free?

You join the waitlist. As soon as someone offers an office on one of your days, it's assigned to you automatically. No need to check back.

Do I lose my office when I offer it?

No. It's only shared on the days you pick. You keep it on every other day.

Who can see our schedule?

Only people with your organization's access code. They can see and change it in the app, so keep the code private.
