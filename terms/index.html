1. What OfficeSwap does

OfficeSwap helps an organization share offices. Office holders can offer their office on days they're away. People without an office can request one for specific days. The app assigns offered offices to requests automatically, in the order requests were made, and keeps a waitlist of requests it can't fill yet.

7. Subscriptions and billing

Joining an organization with its access code is free. Setting up a new organization requires a paid subscription, bought on our website through Stripe, our payment processor.

  • Plans. Starter covers up to 19 offices for $299 a month, Growth covers up to 99 offices for $699 a month, and Enterprise covers 100 or more offices for $1,099 a month, plus any applicable taxes. A plan counts the individual offices on the organization's office list, including hot desks; locations are unlimited. The app won't save an office list larger than the plan allows.
  • Free trial. Each new organization starts with a 30-day free trial. You give a payment method when you start, and it's first charged when the trial ends unless you cancel before then.
  • Renewal. Subscriptions renew automatically each month and are charged in advance until cancelled.
  • Changing or cancelling. You can change plans, update your card, see invoices or cancel anytime at officeswap.co/account. Cancelling stops future charges; the organization keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for. We don't give refunds for partial months, except where the law requires.
  • Failed payments. If a payment fails, we'll retry it. If it still can't be collected, or the subscription ends, the organization's schedule is paused in the app until the subscription is renewed. Its data isn't deleted unless someone with its admin code deletes it.
  • Price changes. We may change prices for future periods. We'll tell the subscriber by email at least 30 days before a change takes effect, and you can cancel before then.

Organizations set up before paid plans began aren't charged and have no office limit.
